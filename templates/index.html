About NeuroKey

NeuroKey is a research-backed screening tool developed at VIT Pune that leverages keystroke dynamics — a non-invasive biometric — to detect subtle motor control changes associated with early Parkinson's disease.

Parkinson's disease affects over 10 million people worldwide. Motor symptoms often appear years before clinical diagnosis. By analyzing the way you type, we can identify patterns that correlate with early neurological changes.

Non-invasive screening

No blood tests, no equipment — just a keyboard and 90 seconds.

10-feature pipeline

Hold time, flight time, rhythm score, IKI coefficient, backspace rate, age, gender — all feed the model.

Ensemble ML model

VotingClassifier combining Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and Logistic Regression with 5-fold cross validation.

Model Pipeline

1

Synthetic Dataset

1,050 subjects generated from published neuroQWERTY and Nature research on PD keystroke biomarkers.

2

Feature Engineering

10 features: mean/std hold, mean/std flight, IKI-CV, rhythm score, backspace rate, hold-to-flight ratio, age, gender.

3

Ensemble Classifier

RF + GradientBoosting + LogisticRegression VotingClassifier. Accuracy 99.5%, AUC 1.0.

4

Real-time Capture

Browser captures raw hold/flight arrays, filters noise, sends to Flask for server-side feature computation.

5

Report Generation

Session-based PDF report with comparison analysis, confidence score, and clinical recommendations.
